IMAN Staff Desk privacy policy

IMAN Cleaning Service LLC operates IMAN Staff Desk. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app. Updated November 5, 2026.

Staff visit planner

Staff sign in to use the visit planner. Planner entries and checklists are saved in protected storage on the device, separated by workspace and excluded from backups. Entries stay separate from scheduled CRM bookings. Signed-in staff can edit or delete their planner visits. Deleting the app removes the data it stores on the device.

Staff and sample workspaces

Authorized staff use an access key to retrieve company bookings and walkthroughs from IMAN’s servers. These can include customer names, contact and service details, schedules, and payment status. The native app holds the staff key and the downloaded records in memory and clears them when you sign out. Checklist progress is stored on the device. Staff CRM administration opens a separately authenticated website; its customer records, messages, calls, attachments, scheduling and payment activities are covered by the IMAN Cleaning Service privacy policy.

Apple App Review signs in with a dedicated key that a separate service hosted on Vercel verifies. This workspace contains only fictional records. Live staff records and staff planner entries are accessible only in the authorized staff workspace. Servers and hosting providers may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure these services.

IMAN marketing messages

Authorized staff can use customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, and communication preferences in the business CRM to send IMAN promotions by email or text. To opt out of promotional emails, use the unsubscribe link in the message. To opt out of promotional texts, reply STOP.

Directions and external sites

If you tap Directions, the app sends that visit’s destination address to Apple Maps. You can use the planner without opening Maps. The native planner has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track people across other companies’ apps or websites.

Questions or requests

To ask about staff or customer data, retention, or deletion, email info@imancleaningservice.com or contact IMAN Cleaning Service. Signed-in users can manage local planner entries on their device.
