Airbnb Turnover Cleaning for NYC Hosts

Iman Cleaning Service LLC provides Airbnb cleaning and short-term rental turnover cleaning for hosts, rental owners, and property managers across New York City. The goal is to help each guest arrive to a space that feels fresh, clean, organized, and ready for their stay.

Turnover cleaning can include bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, floors, trash removal, surface wiping, restocking provided supplies, replacing provided linens, checking visible guest-readiness details, and following your host checklist. The exact scope can be adjusted for studio apartments, multi-bedroom rentals, brownstones, condos, and managed rental units.

Short-Term Rental Cleaning Priorities

Cleanliness affects guest reviews, repeat bookings, and the way your rental is perceived in photos and in person.

  • Guest-ready kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living area, and floor cleaning
  • Trash removal, surface wiping, mirror cleaning, and high-touch area cleaning
  • Changing provided bed sheets and placing provided towels or guest supplies
  • Visual readiness checks for obvious issues before the next guest arrives
  • One-time, same-week, recurring, or property-manager turnover cleaning requests
  • Quote review based on unit size, checkout window, condition, laundry needs, and host checklist

Airbnb Cleaning FAQs

These answers are written directly into the page for customers and search engines.

Do you follow my Airbnb host checklist?

Yes. Share your cleaning checklist, restocking instructions, guest-readiness priorities, and any building access details when requesting a quote.

Do you change sheets and towels?

Yes, bed sheets and towels can be changed when clean linens are provided and the request is included in the cleaning scope.

Can Airbnb cleaning be recurring?

Yes. Hosts and property managers can request recurring turnover cleaning based on booking volume, checkout times, and property needs.

How much does Airbnb cleaning start at?

Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning starts at $150. Final pricing depends on unit size, condition, linen setup, restocking needs, and the turnaround window.
