Overview

Our Details Cleaning service is best for clients who want to choose exactly what areas or tasks need extra attention. If a regular Deep Cleaning does not cover everything you want, but you do not need an extreme or specialty cleaning service, Details Cleaning may be the right option.

You can send us photos and describe your cleaning needs as specifically as possible. This helps us prepare a custom quote and schedule the right team for your appointment.

This service can be booked for a full home or for specific areas such as the kitchen, bathroom, cabinets, closets, windows, baseboards, floors, or any room that needs focused attention.

Best For

Our Detailed Cleaning service is perfect for clients who want complete flexibility and a customized cleaning experience. Instead of cleaning the entire home, you choose the rooms, areas, or tasks that matter most.

Ideal for:

  • Homes that only need specific areas professionally cleaned
  • Clients who want to customize exactly what gets cleaned
  • Kitchens with grease, buildup, or appliances that need extra attention
  • Bathrooms that require intensive scrubbing and sanitizing
  • Interior cabinets, drawers, closets, and pantry organization
  • Baseboards, doors, trim, window sills, and other high-detail areas
  • Interior window cleaning
  • One-room or multi-room detailed cleaning
  • Preparing a home for guests, parties, holidays, or special events
  • Post-party cleanup
  • Move-in and move-out touch-ups
  • Rental properties and Airbnb turnovers needing targeted attention
  • Homes where certain rooms need more work than others
  • Clients who don't need a full Deep Cleaning but want exceptional results in selected areas
  • Anyone looking for a personalized cleaning plan tailored to their home's unique needs

Every Detailed Cleaning service is customized to your priorities and is quoted based on the areas selected, the condition of the home, and the estimated time required.

Details Cleaning Does Not Include

The Details Cleaning quote does not include the following services unless approved separately.

  • Junk removal
  • Furniture removal
  • Construction debris removal
  • Hoarding cleanup
  • Mold removal or mold remediation
  • Pest-related cleanup
  • Biohazard cleanup
  • Human or animal waste cleanup
  • Heavy lifting
  • Moving heavy appliances
  • Unsafe ladder work
  • Carpet shampooing
  • Upholstery shampooing
  • Laundry
  • Dishwashing unless approved
  • Organization unless booked separately
  • Repairs or maintenance work
  • Guaranteed stain removal
  • Permanent buildup removal

Some services may be available separately or may require a custom quote. Please request add-ons before the appointment.

!

Important Note

Details Cleaning is a custom-quoted service. We do our best to complete the agreed cleaning plan, but the final result depends on the condition of the space, the amount of buildup, the requested tasks, and the number of cleaners scheduled.

To help us estimate properly, we may ask for photos before confirming the appointment. If the home's condition is significantly different from what was described, pricing may change, or the appointment may need to be adjusted or rescheduled.
