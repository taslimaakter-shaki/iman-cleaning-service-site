The Bronx cleaning FAQs

Do you work with Bronx landlords and property managers?

Yes — unit turnovers, post-tenant recoveries, and recurring common-area cleaning. Multiple units get one contact and per-unit pricing in writing.

Do you clean private houses as well as apartments?

Yes — from Throgs Neck and Morris Park houses to Riverdale co-op apartments. Crew size scales to the home so visits stay efficient.

Is The Bronx priced differently from Manhattan?

No — pricing is by space and scope, not by borough. Every service is priced from the property and requested scope.
