Overview

Our Standard Cleaning service is best for homes and apartments that are already in good condition and need regular maintenance. This service is ideal for weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly cleaning.

If the home has heavy buildup, excessive dirt, grease, soap scum, clutter, pet hair, or has not been cleaned in a long time, we may recommend Deep Cleaning instead.

Looking for local service? See apartment cleaning in Queens or house cleaning in Jamaica, NY.

General Cleaning Includes

  • Dusting reachable surfaces
  • Vacuuming floors, carpets, and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping hard floors
  • Dry dusting baseboards
  • Removing reachable cobwebs
  • Wiping light switches and door handles
  • Emptying trash bins
  • Replacing trash liners if provided
  • Light straightening of common areas
  • Making beds if clean bedding is provided

We do not move heavy furniture. We clean around furniture and reach underneath where it is safely accessible.

Kitchen Cleaning Includes

  • Wiping countertops
  • Cleaning sink and faucet area
  • Wiping outside of appliances
  • Cleaning stovetop surface
  • Wiping outside of cabinets
  • Wiping tables and visible surfaces
  • Emptying trash
  • Sweeping and mopping floor

Bathroom Cleaning Includes

  • Cleaning and sanitizing toilet
  • Cleaning sink and countertop
  • Cleaning mirrors
  • Cleaning bathtub or shower surface
  • Wiping bathroom fixtures
  • Removing light soap residue
  • Emptying trash
  • Sweeping and mopping floor

Bedrooms & Living Areas Include

  • Dusting furniture and reachable surfaces
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping floors
  • Light straightening
  • Making beds if clean bedding is provided
  • Emptying trash bins

Rate Does Not Include

The Standard Cleaning starting price does not include the following services unless added separately or booked as a different service.

  • Inside refrigerator cleaning
  • Inside oven cleaning
  • Inside cabinets or drawers
  • Interior window cleaning
  • Blind or shutter cleaning
  • Wall washing
  • Laundry
  • Dishwashing
  • Organizing closets, cabinets, or drawers
  • Scrubbing baseboards
  • Heavy grease removal
  • Heavy soap scum removal
  • Heavy dust buildup
  • Moving heavy furniture
  • Post-construction cleaning
  • Mold removal
  • Pest-related cleaning
  • Biohazard cleanup

Some services may be available as add-ons and must be requested before the appointment.
