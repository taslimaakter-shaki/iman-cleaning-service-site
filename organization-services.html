Overview

Our Organization service is designed to help clients declutter, sort, arrange, and create a more functional living space. Whether you need help organizing a closet, bedroom, kitchen, home office, storage area, or apartment, our team helps create a cleaner and easier-to-maintain environment.

This service is perfect for busy clients, small apartments, families, move-in organization, closet organization, and anyone who feels overwhelmed by clutter.

Important Note: Organization is different from cleaning. This service focuses on sorting, arranging, decluttering, and creating systems for your belongings. Cleaning can be booked separately or added if needed.

Home Organizing

Our team helps organize everyday living spaces so your home feels easier to use and maintain. We can help with bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, closets, offices, shelves, drawers, cabinets, and storage areas.

  • Sort visible clutter
  • Organize household items
  • Arrange commonly used items for easy access
  • Create simple systems for daily routines
  • Help make the space feel more open and functional

Decluttering Services

If clutter has built up over time, our team can help you sort through items and decide what to keep, donate, discard, or move to another area.

  • Sort items by category
  • Separate keep, donate, trash, and relocate piles
  • Organize clothes, papers, kitchen items, beauty products, toys, and household items
  • Help reduce visual clutter
  • Create a cleaner and more manageable space

The client is responsible for final decisions about what to keep, donate, or throw away. We do not discard personal belongings without client approval.

Closet Organization

Our closet organization service helps make clothing, shoes, accessories, linens, and personal items easier to find and maintain.

  • Sort clothes by type or category
  • Organize shoes and accessories
  • Fold and arrange items neatly
  • Separate donation or discard items if requested
  • Organize shelves, drawers, and hanging areas
  • Create a more practical closet layout

Apartment Organization

NYC apartments often have limited space. Our apartment organization service helps clients make better use of small spaces, storage areas, closets, cabinets, shelves, and everyday living areas.

  • Maximize small spaces
  • Organize storage areas
  • Improve room flow
  • Reduce clutter in common areas
  • Arrange items based on daily use
  • Help create a more comfortable living space

Home Office & Paper Organization

We can help organize desks, papers, documents, office supplies, books, and work areas so your space feels easier to use.

  • Sort paperwork into categories
  • Organize desk areas
  • Arrange office supplies
  • Organize books or files
  • Reduce clutter from work surfaces
  • Create a simple paper management system

We do not provide legal, financial, or tax advice. Clients are responsible for reviewing important documents before anything is discarded.
