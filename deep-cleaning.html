Overview

Our Deep Cleaning service is designed for homes and apartments that need more attention than a routine Standard Cleaning. We focus on removing built-up dust, dirt, and grime with extra care throughout the home, including kitchens, bathrooms, baseboards, floors, high-touch surfaces, and other commonly used areas.

Deep Cleaning is ideal for homes that haven't been professionally cleaned in a while, homes with noticeable dust or buildup, or anyone looking to refresh their space before beginning recurring cleaning services.

Queens customers can also review our local apartment cleaning services and Jamaica house cleaning service.

If your home has excessive clutter, hoarding conditions, heavy dirt accumulation, pest activity, biohazards, mold, or other situations requiring specialized cleaning, we may recommend a different service or provide a custom quote after evaluating the property.

General Deep Cleaning Includes

  • Detailed dusting of reachable surfaces
  • Vacuuming floors, carpets, and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping hard floors
  • Cleaning baseboards
  • Removing reachable cobwebs
  • Wiping light switches and door handles
  • Wiping commonly touched surfaces
  • Cleaning interior windows where reachable and safe
  • Making beds if clean bedding is provided
  • Emptying trash bins
  • Replacing trash liners if provided
  • Moving light furniture when safe to do so
  • Cleaning under furniture where safely accessible

We do not move heavy furniture, fragile furniture, large appliances, or anything that may cause injury or damage. We clean around furniture and reach underneath where it is safely accessible.

Kitchen Deep Cleaning Includes

  • Cleaning countertops
  • Cleaning sink and faucet area
  • Cleaning stovetop surface
  • Wiping outside of appliances
  • Cleaning behind appliances that are safe to move
  • Cleaning inside microwave
  • Cleaning inside refrigerator
  • Cleaning inside oven
  • Cleaning inside cabinets and drawers
  • Light dishwashing
  • Wiping cabinet doors and drawer fronts
  • Wiping backsplash
  • Wiping tables and visible surfaces
  • Removing light to moderate grease buildup from accessible surfaces
  • Emptying trash
  • Sweeping and mopping floor

Cabinets and drawers should be emptied before the appointment so we can clean the interiors safely. Light dishwashing covers a small load of everyday dishes. We do not clean expensive, fragile, or unsafe dishes without an agreement before the appointment.

Bathroom Deep Cleaning Includes

  • Cleaning and sanitizing toilet
  • Cleaning sink and countertop
  • Cleaning mirrors
  • Cleaning bathtub or shower surface
  • Scrubbing shower walls
  • Scrubbing tub area
  • Wiping bathroom fixtures
  • Removing light to moderate soap residue
  • Cleaning vanity exterior
  • Wiping towel racks and toilet paper holder
  • Emptying trash
  • Sweeping and mopping floor

Heavy soap scum, hard-water stains, rust, mold, old grout discoloration, or permanent stains may not be fully removable. Mold remediation is not included. We clean visible and safely accessible bathroom areas.

Bedrooms & Living Areas Include

  • Detailed dusting of furniture and reachable surfaces
  • Dusting windowsills and reachable ledges
  • Cleaning baseboards
  • Removing reachable cobwebs
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping hard floors
  • Light straightening of visible areas
  • Making beds if clean bedding is provided
  • Emptying trash bins
  • Wiping high-touch areas

Rate Does Not Include

The Deep Cleaning starting price does not include the following services unless added separately or booked as a different service.

  • Inside closets
  • Blind or shutter cleaning
  • Wall washing
  • Laundry
  • Organizing closets, cabinets, or drawers
  • Heavy decluttering
  • Moving heavy furniture
  • Cleaning behind heavy appliances that are unsafe to move
  • Exterior window cleaning
  • High-reach cleaning requiring unsafe ladders
  • Post-construction cleaning
  • Hoarding cleanup
  • Mold removal or mold remediation
  • Pest-related cleaning
  • Biohazard cleanup
  • Human or animal waste cleanup
  • Permanent stain removal

Some services may be available as add-ons and must be requested before the appointment. Additional charges may apply depending on condition, time, and availability.

Important Note

Our Deep Cleaning service is designed to provide a thorough cleaning throughout your home. However, it is not a restoration service and does not include post-construction cleanup, hoarding cleanup, mold remediation, biohazard cleaning, or other specialty cleaning services.

To ensure we schedule the appropriate amount of time and provide an accurate estimate, we may request photos of your home before confirming your appointment. If the actual condition of the property differs significantly from the information provided, additional charges may apply, or the appointment may need to be rescheduled or adjusted to accommodate the required work.
