Overview

Post-Construction Cleaning is designed for properties that need cleaning after renovation, construction, repairs, remodeling, painting, or contractor work. This service focuses on removing construction dust from accessible surfaces, cleaning floors, wiping surfaces, cleaning kitchens and bathrooms, and preparing the property for move-in, staging, or daily use.

Before scheduling, please confirm that construction work is complete or provide accurate information if other workers will still be on-site. If contractors are still working, the cleaning may need to be rescheduled or billed hourly because dust and debris can continue to spread.

Photos are required if you want a flat-rate estimate.

General Cleaning Includes

  • Dusting reachable surfaces
  • Wiping accessible surfaces
  • Vacuuming floors
  • Sweeping floors
  • Mopping hard floors
  • Cleaning baseboards
  • Cleaning inside closets
  • Cleaning inside cabinets
  • Cleaning inside drawers
  • Interior window cleaning where reachable and safe
  • Wiping windowsills and ledges
  • Removing reachable cobwebs
  • Cleaning light switches
  • Cleaning door handles
  • Removing light construction dust from accessible areas
  • Small trash removal
  • Removing up to 1-2 small bags of trash if needed

Standard service includes removing a small amount of trash. Large amounts of construction debris, contractor trash, paint cans, wood, tile, drywall, furniture, boxes, or junk removal are not included unless approved separately.

Kitchen Cleaning Includes

  • Cleaning countertops
  • Cleaning sink and faucet area
  • Cleaning stovetop surface
  • Cleaning inside microwave
  • Cleaning inside refrigerator if requested
  • Cleaning inside oven if requested
  • Wiping outside of appliances
  • Cleaning cabinet exteriors
  • Cleaning inside cabinets
  • Cleaning inside drawers
  • Wiping backsplash
  • Removing light construction dust from reachable kitchen surfaces

Appliances must be empty before interior cleaning. Heavy grease, paint, adhesive, caulk, grout haze, or contractor residue may require extra time and may not be fully removable. We do not move heavy appliances unless approved and safe.

Bathroom Cleaning Includes

  • Cleaning and sanitizing toilet
  • Cleaning sink and countertop
  • Cleaning mirrors
  • Cleaning bathtub or shower surface
  • Wiping bathroom fixtures
  • Cleaning vanity exterior
  • Cleaning inside empty bathroom cabinets and drawers
  • Removing light construction dust from reachable bathroom surfaces
  • Sweeping and mopping floor

Paint residue, grout haze, adhesive, caulk, hard-water stains, rust, mold, or old grout discoloration may not be fully removable. Mold remediation is not included. We clean visible and safely accessible bathroom areas.
