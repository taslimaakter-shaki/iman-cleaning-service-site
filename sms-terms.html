1. Scope and program description

These SMS Terms and Conditions ("SMS Terms") govern transactional and customer-care text messages sent by or on behalf of Iman Cleaning Service LLC ("Iman Cleaning," "we," "us," or "our") through the Iman Cleaning Customer Care and Service Updates program. They supplement our Privacy Policy.

Program messages concern cleaning services that you request or an active customer relationship with us. They may include:

  • quote confirmations, secure quote links, and quote-status updates;
  • appointment availability, selection, and booking confirmations;
  • booking-deposit, payment-status, receipt, and invoice information;
  • appointment reminders and service-related questions;
  • rescheduling, cancellation, refund, and booking-status updates; and
  • responses to customer-care or support requests.

This program is intended for transactional and customer-care messages, not advertising or telemarketing. We will not use consent to this program to send promotional texts. If we introduce a promotional SMS program, we will request separate consent and provide any disclosures required by law.

Program messages are informational. Unless a change is expressly confirmed through our booking system or another authorized written record, a text message does not replace or amend an accepted estimate, Service Agreement, payment term, appointment record, or cancellation policy.

You enroll only when you affirmatively request or agree to receive text messages. Examples include checking an unchecked SMS-consent box and submitting the related form, expressly asking us by telephone, email, chat, or another channel to text you, or initiating a text conversation and requesting a response.

Merely giving us a telephone number does not, by itself, authorize recurring automated text messages. By enrolling, you authorize Iman Cleaning and communications providers acting for us to send program messages to the mobile number you identify. Messages may be sent using automated technology. Your authorization is limited to the customer-care program described in these SMS Terms and does not authorize unrelated marketing.

These SMS Terms do not expand the specific consent disclosure shown when you enroll. If that disclosure authorizes a narrower category of messages, the narrower disclosure controls.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing cleaning services. You may request service and communicate without SMS by emailing Info@imancleaningservice.com or calling 929-803-4053.

Message frequency varies according to your requests, quotes, bookings, and interactions with us. A typical quote or booking may generate a quote link, a booking or payment confirmation, up to two appointment reminders, and additional messages when you contact us or change, reschedule, or cancel a booking. You may receive more messages if you request multiple quotes, make multiple bookings, or continue a support conversation.

We do not guarantee a particular number or delivery time. Longer messages or messages containing links may be delivered as more than one SMS or MMS segment by your carrier.

5. Opting out and revoking consent

You may revoke SMS consent at any time. Reply STOP to a program message, or use any other words that reasonably communicate that you no longer want text messages. Examples include CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, and OPT OUT. STOP is not your only option.

You may also opt out by emailing Info@imancleaningservice.com or calling 929-803-4053 and clearly asking us to stop texts. If you contact us from a different number, identify the mobile number to be removed.

We process valid requests as soon as practicable and no later than the time required by applicable law. We may send one non-promotional message confirming your opt-out. After that confirmation, we will not send further program texts unless you provide fresh affirmative consent. Where supported, you may reply START to enroll again. If you initiate a new text conversation after opting out, that authorizes only replies reasonably related to your new request; it does not by itself reenroll you in recurring automated program messages.

Opting out of SMS does not cancel a quote, appointment, cleaning service, payment obligation, or cancellation policy. It also does not prevent us from using email, telephone calls, or another lawful method to communicate about an active request or transaction.

To stop texts: reply STOP, call 929-803-4053, or email Info@imancleaningservice.com.
