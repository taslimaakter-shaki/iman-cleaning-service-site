5. CLEANING SERVICES

5.01 QUESTION: What cleaning services do you offer?

5.02 QUESTION: What is included in standard cleaning?
ANSWER: Standard cleaning covers routine care of accessible living areas, kitchens, bathrooms, and floors, including dusting, vacuuming, mopping, surface wiping, and emptying bins. Appliance interiors and other extras are not automatically included.

5.03 QUESTION: What is included in deep cleaning?
ANSWER: Deep cleaning gives more attention to built-up dirt, kitchens, bathrooms, baseboards, and accessible surfaces than routine cleaning. Please review the included tasks and any interior-cleaning selections in your written quote before booking.

5.04 QUESTION: What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?
ANSWER: Standard cleaning maintains a home that is already in reasonable condition. Deep cleaning allows more attention to buildup and detailed cleaning; neither is a substitute for restoration, biohazard work, or post-construction cleaning.

5.05 QUESTION: Do you offer move-in cleaning?
ANSWER: Yes. Move-in cleaning is intended for an empty home before you settle in. Use the pricing page and disclose any renovation work or unusual conditions.

5.06 QUESTION: Do you offer move-out cleaning?

ANSWER: No cleaning appointment should be confirmed while an active leak remains unresolved. Arrange the repair and any necessary restoration first, then request review of the remaining cleaning.

ANSWER: We cannot confirm surface cleaning while affected materials are still wet or their condition is uncertain. A restoration professional should assess drying and moisture first.

ANSWER: No. Visible or suspected mold needs appropriate assessment or remediation before our surface-cleaning team can consider work in the area.

22. EMERGENCY AND UNSAFE-SITUATION QUESTIONS

INTERNAL HANDLING — DO NOT READ ALOUD: Stop ordinary intake for an immediate threat. Give the safety instruction first and let the caller contact emergency services. Do not ask for quote details or photos, promise dispatch, or try to manage repairs remotely.

22.01 QUESTION: There is active flooding—what should I do?
ANSWER: If there is immediate danger, move to a safe location and call 911 now. Stay out of floodwater and contact an appropriate restoration provider once safe; do not wait for a cleaning quote.

22.02 QUESTION: Water is touching an electrical outlet.
ANSWER: Stay away from the water and electrical equipment. Move to a safe location and call 911 now if there is an immediate electrical danger; do not touch switches or enter the water.

22.03 QUESTION: The ceiling looks like it might collapse.
ANSWER: Move away from the area and to a safe location, then call 911 if collapse appears possible. Do not go back to take photos or wait for our cleaners.

22.04 QUESTION: There is sewage throughout the property.
ANSWER: Avoid the contaminated area and arrange a qualified contaminated-water restoration service. If anyone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical assistance, move to safety and call 911.

22.05 QUESTION: Someone has been injured.
ANSWER: If the injury is serious or urgent medical help is needed, call 911 now and follow the dispatcher’s instructions. Do not wait for our team or a form submission.

22.06 QUESTION: There is blood or hazardous waste.
ANSWER: Keep away from the affected material and use an appropriate specialist cleanup provider. If there is an injury, exposure, or immediate danger requiring urgent help, call 911 from a safe location.

22.07 QUESTION: There are exposed wires.
ANSWER: Do not touch the wires or nearby water or metal. Stay away and have a qualified electrician address the hazard; if there is immediate danger, move to safety and call 911.

22.08 QUESTION: There is a strong chemical smell.
ANSWER: Move away from the fumes to a safe location. If the smell suggests an immediate danger or anyone is unwell, call 911; do not enter the area to identify or mix chemicals.

22.09 QUESTION: There is a fire or gas smell.
ANSWER: Leave the area immediately and call 911 from a safe location. Do not operate switches, create a flame, or go back inside while waiting for emergency guidance.

22.10 QUESTION: Can your cleaners come immediately and make it safe?
ANSWER: No. Our cleaners are not emergency responders and cannot make a hazardous property safe. If there is immediate danger, move to safety and call 911 before arranging any cleaning.

22.11 QUESTION: Can you call emergency services for me?
ANSWER: I can’t place an emergency call for you through this service. Please call 911 directly now, or ask someone nearby to call if you cannot.
