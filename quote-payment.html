Create a custom payment link in IMAN CRM

All custom payment links now use the protected booking workflow. The customer pays a 25% deposit after reviewing and signing the agreement. The remaining 75% is authorized about 48 hours before the appointment and charged only after the booking is marked completed.

Creating the link in the CRM also ensures the paid customer appears in Bookings and Customers with clock-in, clock-out, completion, additional-work, rescheduling and payment controls.
