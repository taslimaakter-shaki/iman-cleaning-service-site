Frequently asked questions

Questions before you book?

What is included in a cleaning?

Your cleaning scope depends on the service and options you select. You can review the included work before booking.

Do you bring cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring professional cleaning products and equipment unless you ask us to use specific products.

How do quotes and payments work?

You’ll see or receive your price before booking. A 25% deposit reserves the appointment, and the remaining balance is due after cleaning.

Where do you provide cleaning service?

We serve Brooklyn, Queens, and Manhattan. Enter your ZIP code to confirm availability for your address.

What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning supports routine upkeep. Deep cleaning is designed for heavier buildup and areas that require more detailed attention.
