Company: Iman Cleaning Service LLC
Client: The person booking, scheduling, purchasing, or receiving services

By booking, scheduling, purchasing, or receiving services from Iman Cleaning Service LLC ("Company"), the Client agrees to the following Terms and Conditions.

!Important · Please Read Before Payment

Your time and budget stay in your control

Your estimate is based on the information and details provided before your appointment. We do our best to estimate the required cleaning time accurately; however, the full condition and scope of work can only be confirmed once our cleaning team sees the property in person.

Property Conditions

If the actual condition, level of buildup, or work required differs from what could reasonably be determined beforehand, the service may require additional time.

Approval Before Extra Time

Your cleaning team will work within the maximum labor-hours shown in your estimate. The service may be completed sooner. If additional time is recommended, we’ll explain why and get your approval before continuing.

No Surprise Charges

The total price for any additional time will be shown for your approval and will never be added without your permission.

This is a maximum estimate, not a guaranteed duration. Your cleaning may be completed sooner.

Cleaner Break Time

For longer appointments, our cleaning team may take reasonable meal or rest breaks. Break time does not count toward the maximum labor-hours included in your estimate and will not result in an additional charge.

1. Services

The Company provides residential and commercial cleaning services, including but not limited to standard cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in cleaning, move-out cleaning, Airbnb turnover cleaning, office cleaning, and related cleaning services.

The scope of work shall be limited to the services outlined in the estimate, invoice, booking confirmation, or written agreement.

Floor scrubbing is not included in any service rate unless it is expressly included in a separate written estimate or agreement.

Hourly Cleaning Minimum Charge

Hourly Cleaning Service has a four-work-hour minimum. The Client agrees to pay for all work-hours booked, including the four-work-hour minimum, even if the requested cleaning is completed in less time.

Booked work-hours represent the combined labor of the assigned cleaners. For example, four work-hours may be provided by two cleaners working for approximately two hours each. Unused booked time is not prorated, refunded, credited, or transferred when the service is completed sooner than the number of work-hours reserved.

2. Deposit and Remaining Balance Authorization

For an Instant Pricing appointment, a 25% deposit is charged immediately to secure the appointment. Other written quotes follow the payment schedule shown on their approval and checkout pages.

By booking an Instant Pricing appointment, the Client authorizes the Company and its payment processor to save the selected payment method and place a temporary authorization hold for the remaining 75% approximately 48 hours before the appointment.

The remaining 75% is captured only after the scheduled service is completed. An authorization can temporarily reduce the Client’s available credit or funds even though it is not yet a completed charge.

If the authorization is declined or requires additional authentication, the Client must provide another approved payment method before service proceeds.

3. Cancellation Policy

If the Client cancels less than 48 hours before the scheduled appointment, 25% of the total service price is non-refundable.

If the Client cancels at least 48 hours before the scheduled appointment, no cancellation fee will apply and the booking deposit will be fully refundable, except when an approved late reschedule has already triggered the cancellation policy below.

4. Rescheduling Policy

All appointment changes require Company approval and availability. The original appointment remains confirmed until the change is approved. One approved reschedule requested at least 48 hours before the appointment is free. Second and subsequent requests require direct contact and approval.

Requests received less than 48 hours before the appointment incur a fee of 10% of the booked cleaning price, including tax and excluding separate rush or additional-work charges. This fee is separate from the cleaning payment and is charged when the change is approved.

If a booking rescheduled within that window is later canceled, the cancellation fee remains 25% of the booked cleaning price regardless of the new date. Paid late-rescheduling fees are credited toward that fee; for example, a paid 10% fee leaves 15% to retain from the deposit. Rescheduling does not restore free cancellation.

The Client authorizes disclosed and accepted booking-related fees to the same saved payment method used for the 25% deposit. Cancellation fees are retained from payments already collected where possible; amounts already paid are not charged twice. A failed or unverified rescheduling payment does not confirm a new appointment. If the Company cancels, paid rescheduling fees are refunded.

5. Payment Terms

Instant Pricing appointments require the 25% deposit before confirmation. A temporary authorization hold for the remaining 75% is placed approximately 48 hours before the appointment. It is not a completed charge, and the balance becomes due when service is completed. Other written quotes follow the payment schedule presented at checkout.

Additional labor or services are never charged without the Client’s approval and must be paid before the additional work begins unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Failure to make payment when due may result in collection efforts and additional fees permitted by law.

6. Estimates and Price Adjustments

All estimates are based on information provided by the Client.

The Company reserves the right to adjust pricing if:

  • the property size differs from what was disclosed;
  • additional rooms or areas are discovered;
  • excessive dirt, buildup, pet hair, clutter, or other conditions require additional labor;
  • the actual condition of the property differs materially from photographs, videos, or descriptions provided by the Client.

The Company may pause or discontinue service until revised pricing is approved.

7. Access to Property

The Client is responsible for providing safe and timely access to the property, accurate entry instructions, any building authorization required for entry, and a work area where the scheduled service can be performed.

If the team arrives as scheduled but cannot begin or continue because access is unavailable, entry instructions are incorrect, a building refuses entry, required utilities are unavailable, unsafe or undisclosed conditions prevent service, or the Client otherwise prevents the scheduled work, the Company will make a reasonable attempt to contact the Client and, when reasonably possible, allow a 15-minute grace period.

If the issue is not resolved, the appointment will be treated as a customer-caused same-day cancellation. The 25% booking deposit is non-refundable, and the Client authorizes an additional charge equal to 25% of the scheduled service price to the saved payment method, for a total charge of 50% of the scheduled service price.

This charge does not apply when the Company or its team caused the inability to perform the scheduled service.

8. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • provide accurate information regarding the condition of the property;
  • provide access to running water and electricity;
  • secure pets;
  • secure valuables and important documents;
  • remove excessive clutter that would interfere with cleaning.

Failure to satisfy these requirements may result in additional charges, modification of services, rescheduling, or cancellation.

9. Condition of Property

The Client agrees to accurately disclose any conditions that may affect the cleaning service, including but not limited to:

  • excessive clutter;
  • hoarding conditions;
  • mold;
  • pest infestations;
  • human or animal waste;
  • smoke damage;
  • biohazards;
  • construction debris;
  • unsafe environments;
  • any condition requiring specialized cleaning procedures.

If the actual condition of the property differs materially from information provided during booking, the Company reserves the right to:

  • adjust pricing;
  • modify the scope of work;
  • reschedule the appointment;
  • refuse service;
  • cancel the appointment.

If the Company determines that the property presents safety concerns, health hazards, or conditions outside the Company's normal cleaning scope, service may be refused or terminated immediately.

Deposits remain subject to the cancellation and rescheduling policies.

10. Clutter Policy

Excessive clutter must be removed before the scheduled cleaning appointment.

The Company reserves the right to charge additional fees, exclude certain areas from service, reschedule the appointment, or cancel service if excessive clutter prevents normal cleaning operations.

11. Pet Policy

Clients must secure aggressive or disruptive pets before service begins.

The Company reserves the right to refuse service if pets create unsafe working conditions.

Additional charges may apply for excessive pet hair, pet waste, or pet-related cleaning conditions.

12. Arrival Window

The Company provides estimated appointment times only.

Actual arrival times may vary due to traffic, weather, prior appointments, emergencies, staffing issues, or other circumstances beyond the Company's control.

Such delays shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

13. Limitations of Cleaning Services

The Company does not guarantee removal of:

  • permanent stains;
  • rust stains;
  • hard water stains;
  • mold staining;
  • paint stains;
  • odors;
  • smoke damage;
  • pet odors;
  • discoloration;
  • wear and tear.

Possible mold or mildew pricing applies only to cleaning accessible surface-level mold or mildew disclosed during booking. It does not include mold inspection, testing, remediation, concealed growth, removal of contaminated building materials, or treatment of hazardous mold conditions. If the condition cannot be safely handled as ordinary surface cleaning, the Company may stop or modify the service and recommend an appropriate specialist.

Cleaning services improve cleanliness but do not restore damaged surfaces or materials to new condition.

The Company shall not be responsible for damage resulting from:

  • existing damage;
  • normal wear and tear;
  • improperly installed fixtures;
  • loose tiles;
  • loose flooring;
  • loose countertops;
  • damaged blinds;
  • aged or brittle materials;
  • manufacturer defects;
  • deteriorated caulking or grout.

The Company may document pre-existing damage before, during, or after service.

15. Valuables

The Client is solely responsible for securing:

  • cash;
  • jewelry;
  • firearms;
  • collectibles;
  • electronics;
  • important documents;
  • other valuables.

The Company shall not be liable for loss or damage to unsecured valuables.

16. Photos & Videos

As part of our standard service process, Iman Cleaning Service LLC may take before, during, and after photos or videos of the areas being serviced.

Service Documentation & Quality Control

Photos and videos may be taken to document property conditions, verify completed work, maintain quality standards, and address any service-related concerns.

Marketing Use

We may also use selected photos or videos of our cleaning work for marketing purposes, including our website, social media, Google Business Profile, and advertisements.

Your privacy is important to us. Customers will not be intentionally photographed or recorded, and we will not publish the property address or other identifying information. We will also make reasonable efforts to avoid capturing personal or sensitive information.

If you do not want photos or videos taken, or do not want them used for marketing, please notify us before your service begins. Otherwise, we will proceed with our standard photo and video process.

17. Satisfaction Guarantee

If the Client believes an area covered by the agreed scope of work was missed, the Client must notify the Company within 24 hours of service completion.

The Company shall be given a reasonable opportunity to inspect and, if appropriate, re-clean the affected area.

The Client agrees that a re-clean shall be the exclusive remedy for cleaning-related complaints.

Refunds are not guaranteed and are issued solely at the Company's discretion.

18. Damage Claims

Any claim for property damage must be submitted in writing within 24 hours of service completion.

The Client must provide:

  • photographs;
  • description of the alleged damage;
  • reasonable access for inspection.

Failure to report alleged damage within 24 hours constitutes a waiver of the claim to the fullest extent permitted by law.

The Client remains responsible for payment for all services rendered.

If the Company prevails in a chargeback dispute, the Client agrees to reimburse any fees, collection costs, administrative costs, attorney's fees, and expenses incurred by the Company to the extent permitted by law.
