Frequently asked questions

Staten Island cleaning FAQs

Do you really cover all of Staten Island?

Yes — North Shore to Tottenville. Driveway parking actually makes Staten Island our smoothest borough for scheduling and equipment.

Can you clean basements and garages?

Yes — finished basements are included in whole-home scope, and unfinished basements or garages can be added to any quote. Just mention them in your request.

Do larger houses cost more than apartments?

Pricing follows space and scope: a full house takes a bigger crew than a one-bedroom. You'll have the exact figure before booking — we confirm the exact quote from the property and requested scope.
